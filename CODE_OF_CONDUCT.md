# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body
size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback (this is a code-review project — review
  the work, never the person; assume competence and good intent).
- Focusing on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, insulting or derogatory comments, personal or political
attacks, publishing others' private information, and other conduct reasonably considered
inappropriate.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive or unacceptable behavior may be reported to the maintainer via a GitHub
Security Advisory or the contact on the maintainer's GitHub profile. All complaints will be
reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.

For the full text and enforcement guidelines, see
<https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>.
